Reminders

  • My reminders don't arrive. First check that notifications are allowed: Settings › Notifications in Habitpup shows whether they are on, with a button to turn them on. Then check the phone:
    • iPhone: Settings › Notifications › Habitpup › Allow Notifications. A Focus mode (Sleep, Work, Do Not Disturb) can hold them back; add Habitpup to the Focus's allowed apps if you want them.
    • Android: Settings › Apps › Habitpup › Notifications on. Some phones (Samsung, Xiaomi, Huawei, OnePlus and others) stop apps in the background to save battery: set Habitpup's battery use to "Unrestricted" or "No restrictions" and turn off "Pause app activity if unused".
  • A reminder came a few minutes late. On Android, reminders use the system's battery-friendly alarms, which can arrive a few minutes after the set time.
  • No reminder on some days. That's on purpose: Habitpup skips a reminder when the habit isn't due that day, when it's already done, on a rest day, or when a weekly goal is already met. Open the app once after travelling to a new time zone so reminders follow the new local time.

Backup and restore

  • Where are my backups? Habitpup makes a backup every day you open it. On iPhone it goes to the Habitpup folder in iCloud Drive (open the Files app to see it). On Android it goes to the app's storage, and Android Auto Backup copies the app to your Google account if backup is on in your phone's settings. Settings › Backup and import shows when the last backup was made, and you can tap "Back up now".
  • Extra copies. In Settings › Backup and import you can also connect Google Drive (Android), copy backups to a folder you choose, or export a file to save or send anywhere.
  • Moving to a new phone. On iPhone, sign in to the same iCloud account with iCloud Drive on, install Habitpup and tap "Restore my habits from a backup" on the welcome screen (or Settings › Backup and import › Restore). On Android, restore your phone from your Google backup, or connect the same Google Drive account and restore from there. Any phone: export a file on the old one, open it on the new one with Restore from a file.
  • Restoring replaces what's in the app now. Habitpup keeps a safety copy of the current data first, so you can go back.

Moving from HabitKit or Loop

  • HabitKit: in HabitKit, open Settings › Backup & Restore › Export a copy and save the .json file. In Habitpup, open Settings › Backup and import › Import from HabitKit or Loop (or "Switching from HabitKit or Loop" during setup), choose HabitKit and pick the file.
  • Loop Habit Tracker: in Loop, open Settings › Export full backup. In Habitpup choose Loop and pick the .zip file (or Habits.csv).
  • You see a preview before anything changes. If a habit already exists, choose "Keep mine" or "Replace". Your history, goals and colours come along; imported habits don't count against the free limit.
  • The file is read on your phone and never uploaded.

Habitpup Pro

  • Restore purchases. On a new phone or after reinstalling, open Settings › About › Restore purchases (or "Restore" on the Pro screen), signed in with the same Apple ID or Google account you paid with.
  • Change or cancel a subscription. In the app: Settings › About › Manage subscription. Or iPhone: Settings › your name › Subscriptions. Android: Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. Cancel at least 24 hours before the trial or period ends to avoid the next charge.
  • Refunds are handled by Apple (reportaproblem.apple.com) or Google Play under their policies.
  • What happens if Pro ends? Nothing is deleted. All habits and history stay; you just can't add habits beyond the free limit.

Your data

  • Delete all my data. Habitpup has no account. Settings › About › Delete all my data erases every habit, check-in and note on the device, along with the app's automatic backups. Files you exported and copies in Google Drive or a folder you chose stay there until you delete them yourself. Uninstalling the app also removes the data on the device.
  • Turn off anonymous usage data. Settings › About › Share anonymous usage data. Read more in the Privacy policy.
