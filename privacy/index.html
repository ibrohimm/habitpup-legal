Habitpup

Privacy policy

Effective and last updated: November 21, 2026

Habitpup ("the app") is made by BrightApps, an individual developer (Ibrohim Movlonov). This policy explains what the app does with information. The short version: your habits stay on your device. No account, no ads, no tracking, nothing sold.

What stays on your device

Everything you put into Habitpup is stored only in the app's database on your phone or tablet. We have no server and no copy of it:

  • Your habits, goals, check-ins, notes, rest days, reminders and categories.
  • Your settings (theme, language, day start, widget look and so on).
  • Reminders are local notifications scheduled by your phone. Nothing is sent over the internet to deliver them.
  • Home-screen widgets read a small summary of today's habits that the app shares with the widget on your device only.
  • Importing from HabitKit or Loop Habit Tracker reads the file you choose, on your device. The file is not uploaded anywhere.

Backups you control

Backups are copies of your own data, kept in places that belong to you. We can't see or open them.

  • iPhone and iPad: automatic daily backups are saved as files in Habitpup's folder in your iCloud Drive (or on the device if iCloud Drive is off). Apple stores them under its own privacy policy.
  • Android Auto Backup: if backup is on in your phone's settings, Android copies the app's database to your Google account, as it does for other apps. Google stores it under its own privacy policy.
  • Google Drive (Android, optional): if you connect Google Drive in Settings › Backup and import, the app asks Google for access to a private app folder in your own Drive and copies daily backups there. The app can see only its own backup files there, never your other files. Your Google account's email is shown in the app so you know which account is connected; it stays on your device. You can disconnect at any time.
  • A folder you choose (optional): backups can also be copied to a folder you pick, for example on a cloud drive app.
  • Export and import: you can export a backup file and share it wherever you like, and restore one later. Where it goes is up to you.

What we collect, and only if you allow it

To fix crashes and learn which features help, release versions of the app send anonymous usage and crash data through Google Firebase (Analytics and Crashlytics). This is on by default and you can turn it off at any time in Settings › About › Share anonymous usage data. When it is off, nothing is sent. It includes:

  • App events such as "a habit was checked in", "a backup was made", "the Pro screen was shown" or "an import finished", with details like the kind of habit (yes/no, count or amount) or how many habits were imported.
  • Crash reports with technical details of what went wrong (Firebase Crashlytics).
  • A random app instance ID created by Firebase, device model, system version, app version, language, and an approximate country derived by Google from the connection.

Never included: the names you give your habits, your notes, your check-in history, your contacts, location or any advertising identifier. This data is not linked to your name, email or Apple / Google account. We don't use it for advertising, we don't track you across other apps or websites, and we don't sell or share it with anyone except the service providers below.

Purchases

Habitpup Pro is bought through the App Store or Google Play; they handle payment, and we never see your card or account details. To unlock Pro on your devices, RevenueCat receives a random anonymous app user ID and the purchase receipt from the store, and tells the app whether Pro is active.

Service providers

  • Google Firebase Analytics and Crashlytics (anonymous usage and crash data): firebase.google.com/support/privacy
  • RevenueCat (purchase receipts): revenuecat.com/privacy
  • Apple (App Store payments, iCloud Drive backups): apple.com/legal/privacy
  • Google (Google Play payments, Android Auto Backup, Google Drive backups you turn on): policies.google.com/privacy

Children

Habitpup has no ads and no social features and is safe for all ages, but it is not directed at children under 13 and does not knowingly collect personal information from them.

Keeping and deleting data

  • Delete all data: Settings › About › Delete all my data erases every habit, check-in and note on the device, along with the app's automatic backups. Files you exported, copies in a folder you chose and backups in Google Drive stay where they are until you delete them there.
  • Deleting the app removes everything stored on the device. Android Auto Backup copies are managed in your Google account; iCloud Drive files in the Files app.
  • Anonymous usage data is kept by Firebase for up to 14 months, crash reports for up to 90 days. Because it is not linked to you, we can't look it up by name or email; turning the switch off stops any new data.

Your rights

Depending on where you live (for example under the GDPR or CCPA), you can ask to access, correct or delete data about you. Your habit data is already fully in your hands on your device. For anything else, email us and we will help.

Changes

If this policy changes, we will update the date above and, for important changes, tell you in the app.
