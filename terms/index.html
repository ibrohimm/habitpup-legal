The app

Habitpup is a habit tracker. The free plan includes up to 6 habits, 1 reminder per habit, basic stats and the Today widget, with no ads. Habitpup Pro adds unlimited habits, up to 3 reminders per habit, 2 shields a week, Insights stories (week, month and year review) and the One habit and Last 5 days widgets. Backups, export and import are free for everyone.
